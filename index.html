1K Upgrade Strategy Guide

Six tactics to tilt the odds in your favor

⏰

Request Immediately

United 1K members clear upgrades at T-24 hours before departure. Submit your upgrade request as soon as you book — the earlier you are on the waitlist, the better your position. Don't wait until check-in.

🌡

Book Higher Fare Class

Fare class determines your waitlist priority. Y and B class tickets give you top-tier upgrade priority, well above discounted economy fares. That extra cost per ticket can mean the difference between Polaris and coach.

📅

Fly Midweek

Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday flights consistently have lower premium cabin demand. Business travelers dominate Mondays and Fridays, filling upgrade lists fast. A midweek departure dramatically improves your odds.

🍁

Pick Shoulder Season

May and October–November are the sweet spots for international upgrades. Summer (June–August) and major holidays pack premium cabins with revenue passengers. Flying during shoulder season means fewer competitors on the upgrade list.

✈

Choose the Right Plane

The 777-300ER has 60 Polaris seats — twice as many as some 767 variants. More Polaris seats means more upgrade inventory. When two flights serve the same route, always pick the widebody with the larger premium cabin.

🔍

Check PZ Inventory First

Before spending PlusPoints, look up PZ fare bucket availability on ExpertFlyer. PlusPoints upgrades require open PZ space. If PZ is closed days out, save your points for a better flight — they're too valuable to waste on a dead waitlist.
